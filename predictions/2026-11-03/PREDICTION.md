## How it was made
Senate: time-weighted averages of nonpartisan general-election polls (NYT poll database, Electoral-vote.com where the Times has none), blended with a fundamentals estimate (state partisan lean, incumbency, national generic ballot), then 20,000 simulations with a shared national error and fat-tailed state errors. House: each district's 2024 presidential lean shifted by the national generic-ballot average, blended with district polls, 20,000 simulations.

The fundamentals blend and the 30-day undecided-voter adjustment passed leave-one-cycle-out backtests on 2018-2024 Senate races; other adjustments that were tried and failed are listed in the page's "Tested and left out" section. Error sizes are rough: four backtest cycles.

Data: The New York Times poll database; Electoral-vote.com; FiveThirtyEight (ABC News) archive via the Internet Archive; MIT Election Data and Science Lab; The Downballot (district results on 2026 lines); NOAA, ALFRED, Google Trends, US Census Bureau ACS.
